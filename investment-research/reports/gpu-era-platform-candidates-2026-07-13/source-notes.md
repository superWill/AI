## Decision frame

[FRAME] “Microsoft-type potential” means the ability to control the developer interface, application platform, enterprise distribution, switching costs, and recurring monetization layer.

[FRAME] “Intel-type potential” means the ability to establish a broadly accessible compute architecture through silicon/system scale, roadmap execution, software support, supply, and merchant availability.

[FRAME] Scores are ordinal analyst judgments from 0 to 10; they are not market-share forecasts, valuation targets, or probabilities.

## Scoring rubric

[FRAME] Microsoft-type score weights: developer ecosystem 30%, enterprise/application distribution 25%, interface control 20%, switching costs 15%, monetization breadth 10%.

[FRAME] Intel-type score weights: compute architecture 30%, deployment scale 25%, system/network integration 15%, roadmap execution 15%, merchant accessibility 15%.
